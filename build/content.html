📜 A short history of Sheffield

Seven hills and five rivers. Sheffield grew where the Sheaf meets the Don, among steep hills and fast rivers that later powered hundreds of water wheels. Chaucer's Reeve in the Canterbury Tales carries a "Sheffield thwitel", a knife, so the town's blades were famous by the 1300s.

Little mesters. For centuries cutlery was made by small independent workshops, the "little mesters", grinding blades on water-powered wheels in the valleys. Shepherd Wheel and Abbeydale Industrial Hamlet still show how they worked.

Steel City. Benjamin Huntsman perfected crucible steel here in the 1740s, and Thomas Boulsover invented Sheffield Plate. In 1856 Henry Bessemer's converter brought mass-produced steel, and in 1913 Harry Brearley discovered stainless steel in a Sheffield lab. The city made armour, rails and cutlery for the world, and forged guns and steel through two world wars.

Football's home town. Sheffield FC, founded in 1857, is the world's oldest football club, and Bramall Lane is the oldest major stadium still in use. The Crucible has hosted the World Snooker Championship since 1977.

Today the steelworks have mostly gone, but the city has reinvented itself around its universities, music (Pulp, the Arctic Monkeys, the Human League) and its pubs. It is often called the UK's real-ale capital, and with a third of the city inside the Peak District it is one of the greenest cities in Europe.

🍺 The Valley of Beer

Sheffield has dozens of independent breweries and a remarkable number of real-ale pubs, and it has been called the best beer city in the world. The heart of it is Kelham Island, the old industrial quarter along the River Don, where Victorian workers' pubs sit beside brewery taps.

CAMRA's 2026 survey of 191 local venues found the average pint of cask ale here costs £4.46, still cheaper than most cities.

🚶 Kelham Island ale trail

A walk of about 1.5 km with plenty of stops, from the centre out to Kelham Island and back to the station. Tap a name to see each pub.

Pace yourself with halves, and eat at Cutlery Works or the Fat Cat on the way round.

🔪 Made in Sheffield

  • Henderson's Relish: the city's spicy, vinegary sauce, made here since 1885. Locals say it beats Worcestershire sauce.
  • Stainless steel was discovered here in 1913. Look for "Made in Sheffield" stamped on cutlery.
  • "Ey up, love", hello, and "mardy", grumpy, are as Sheffield as they are Nottingham.
